# A little space for your next idea

Notes, plans, and the things you want to remember — kept in your own Markdown files.

## Start with a thought

Great writing often starts with a small observation. Give it a heading, follow the next idea, and let the structure emerge as you go.

> Keep your files close. Keep your focus on the words.

## Make it your own

- [x] Open a folder and collect your notes
- [x] Switch between visual editing and Markdown source
- [ ] Find a theme that feels like home
- [ ] Turn a rough draft into something worth sharing

## A simple writing routine

| Moment | What to do | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capture | Write the idea before it disappears | Inbox |
| Connect | Add a link, a question, or an example | Notes |
| Publish | Review the draft and export a copy | Writing |

## From notes to knowledge

Use headings to find your way, links to connect ideas, and local history to revisit earlier versions. AI is there when you want a second perspective.
