# A little room to think

Good writing starts with a little space. Gather your thoughts, find the thread, and let the words take shape.

> Keep the tools close. Keep the ideas closer.

## From a thought to a draft

Start with a sentence, a sketch of an idea, or a question worth exploring. **The structure can come later.**

- Capture the detail you do not want to forget.
- Connect it to a note, a reference, or a new possibility.
- Refine the story until every paragraph has a purpose.
